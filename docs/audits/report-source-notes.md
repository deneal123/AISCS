# Primary routes retained from the former chronological report

These three routes were present only in the former long validation report at the 2026-10-01 consolidation. They are retained here with the original bounded reading; they do not establish clinical transfer or data access.

- [eLife 76574](https://elifesciences.org/articles/76574), source `S827`: mechanically evoked c4da calcium and a separate larval rolling assay. The assays do not establish subjective pain, a human ECAP mapping, or clinical response.
- [eLife 85760](https://elifesciences.org/articles/85760), source `S828`: thermally evoked Goro calcium and separate protective behavior. The same transfer limits apply.
- [Neuron article DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2023.10.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.10.021): an acute comparison of ECAP and paresthesia at 150 Hz versus 1 kHz in one person. The checked publisher supplement offered no longitudinal linked pain outcome dataset; direct full text access was blocked in that review.

The canonical source cards and [human ECAP access audit](../../data/audits/ecap-scs/human-ecap-scs-access-audit.json) remain authoritative for current claim wording and access status.
