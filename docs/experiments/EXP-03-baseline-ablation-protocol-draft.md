## 1. Question and boundary

Test whether proposed connectome-constrained pretraining adds value to a human ECAP modeling task beyond human-only learning and matched synthetic/null controls. The machine-contract physical observation target is electrically recruited population activity passed through conduction, volume-conduction, electrode, artifact, and acquisition operators, evaluated on unseen participants, configurations, and stimulation settings. This TODO EXP-03 protocol compares arms around that operator and the downstream task; it is not a direct pain inference. STOP-H2-B rejects the tested transfer hypothesis if it fails to improve the pre-selected participant-level metric over human-only, naive-synthetic, and randomized-connectome controls, or worsens calibration.

This is a comparison protocol, not the statistical analysis plan. EXP-04 must pre-register the single primary endpoint, participant-level inference, multiplicity handling, confidence intervals, and calibration decision. EXP-03 freezes arm construction, data exposure, and the shared measurement pipeline after EXP-01/02 resolve their inputs.

## 2. Comparison arms

| Arm | Pretraining / initialization | Role and matching rule |
|---|---|---|
| Complete model | Drosophila-derived representation, then authorized human training data; connectome-constrained dynamics | Candidate transfer arm. Use the EXP-01 locked input and EXP-02 observation operator. |
| Human-only | Authorized human training partition only; no synthetic or fly-derived pretraining | Primary no-transfer baseline. Same downstream architecture, human examples, preprocessing, optimizer and update budget. |
| Naive synthetic | Synthetic activity with no connectome constraint | Tests generic synthetic-data benefit. Match sequence length, modality, scale, label/task distribution, sample count and augmentation to the complete arm where those quantities are defined. Generator is a declared proposal, not an established literature standard. |
| Degree-preserving rewired ensemble | EXP-01 graph nulls; preserve each node's in/out degree and self-loop status, reject duplicate edges; shared-from-scratch initialization paired with the complete arm | Main topology null. Use multiple independently rewired graphs and paired seeds. S741 (arXiv:2604.04033, Methods §§4.1–4.4, control ladder §5.3, results §§6.2–6.5) motivates this control: an apparent topology benefit can diminish with shared initialization and degree-preserving rewiring. Its five-graph × three-seed ensemble is a precedent, not a sample-size prescription. The null also leaves clustering, motifs, spatial and spectral properties unmatched and remains partly correlated with the original graph; report those limits. |
| Shuffled dynamics | Same graph and model; a temporal-order null with declared invariants | Required by the TODO and hypothesis, but no reviewed primary source defines a connectome-constrained ECAP shuffle. Do not treat a generic independent time permutation as interchangeable with a null preserving instantaneous population statistics. Candidate operator: shuffle neuron labels independently per time bin (preserves per-timestep population distribution and instantaneous population co-activation, but destroys each neuron's trajectory); alternatively, use a per-neuron phase surrogate if the target requires preserving spectra. Select one only after manipulation checks and EXP-01 review. |
| No-pretraining | Random initialization, then authorized human training partition | Diagnostic to separate pretraining from architecture and downstream fitting. Keep the initialization seed paired with the human-only arm where feasible. |
| Naive random graph (optional secondary) | Random graph without degree constraints | Weak diagnostic only. Do not treat it as a substitute for the degree-preserving ensemble because node-degree changes confound topology. |
| No-physical-operator (optional diagnostic) | Same recruited activity, bypass ECAP forward/measurement operator | Pipeline negative control only; outputs are not comparable ECAP waveforms and cannot enter the primary metric. |

If the scientific contract or EXP-01 yields a different control definition, resolve the conflict before locking the protocol. In particular, “random-connectome” in brief TODO wording means the degree-preserving rewired ensemble for the primary topology test, consistent with the local S741 audit and novelty landscape. The graph null and dynamics null are separate factors; the main mechanism comparison should cross real versus degree-preserving graph with ordered versus shuffled dynamics, rather than substitute one control for the other.

## 3. Paired design and leakage control

1. Use one immutable participant-grouped split manifest, keyed by cohort family and stable pseudonymous participant ID. Every pulse, sweep, session, visit, configuration and setting for a participant stays in one partition. Inner tuning folds must also be participant-disjoint. Keep artifact templates, PCA, clustering, normalization, feature selection, thresholds, missingness rules, stopping and tuning inside training/validation participants; do not use test participants, including unlabeled recordings, for adaptation.
2. Test on participants and electrode configurations/settings absent from fitting. Hold out participants and setting cells as separate axes for claims about unseen configurations. Prefer an external cohort only after owner-confirmed data access, participant linkage, consent scope and field availability. Repeated pulses or curves are not independent participants. Audit rule FT-06 and the leakage review of S767/S787/S765/S789/S105 reinforce this grouping requirement; S105's waveform-level folds/all-waveform PCA and reused trial cohorts illustrate leakage risks. Gmel et al. 2023 (DOI 10.3389/fnins.2023.1297814) explicitly identifies its frequency-effect results as previously published in S789 and reports the same 20-patient study cohort, so these two reports are one cohort family. Published sample counts do not supply a usable project dataset.
3. Pair random seeds across arms. For connectome-constrained, degree-preserving and shuffled-dynamics arms, initialize corresponding trainable parameters identically from scratch, with the same model architecture, parameter count, optimizer, schedule, batch construction, number of examples and update budget. Use the seed derivation and software/data/model version locks in EXP-01 once verified. Human-only and naive-synthetic controls must receive the same downstream budget; document any unavoidable exposure mismatch instead of implying exact equality.
4. Freeze input units, waveform/polarity handling, artifact removal, filter, sampling rate, detection floor, feature extraction and missingness codes once under EXP-02. Apply them unchanged across primary arms. Keep source-specific windows distinct where acquisition protocols differ; do not harmonize unlike measurements silently.
5. Hash and retain the split manifest, eligible participant/configuration counts, source-data versions, graph and rewiring seeds, model/configuration, preprocessing and scoring code, environment, and per-arm training exposure. Do not begin model comparisons until the data owner authorizes the actual linked recordings and the test partition is locked.

## 4. Outputs, comparison and falsification

For each held-out participant/configuration, produce the same source-aware outputs from EXP-02: waveform/morphology, N1 and later latency where measurable, detection/classifiability and explicit missingness, recruitment response versus stimulation settings, and artifact residual/SNR. Do not infer recruited axon counts uniquely from waveform amplitude; the forward problem is not identifiable without additional constraints. Keep the no-operator diagnostic outside waveform comparisons.

EXP-04 selects one primary participant-level endpoint and its uncertainty/calibration procedure before test access. Report each arm's performance on identical held-out units, paired arm differences and calibration; show participant denominators and excluded/missing observations. Numeric acceptance bounds remain unset until authorized human reference signals and locked preprocessing exist. Never choose thresholds from abstracts or tune them against the held-out set.

Falsify or withhold the transfer claim if any of the following occurs:

- the complete arm does not improve the pre-registered primary participant-level metric over human-only, naive-synthetic and the degree-preserving null, under EXP-04's decision rule;
- calibration worsens under the pre-registered definition, or required physical/signal characteristics fail their human-derived bounds;
- apparent gains disappear on unseen participants, configurations or settings, or depend on train/test leakage, unmatched update budgets, unpaired initialization, or selected seeds;
- naive synthetic or degree-preserving controls match the complete arm within the pre-registered uncertainty/equivalence rule;
- plausible anatomy, conductivity, contact, waveform or artifact alternatives fit similarly while implying materially different latent recruitment; in that case report non-identifiability and do not claim recovered physiology.

A negative result is reportable. No result here establishes pain measurement, analgesia, patient benefit, or clinical SCS response.

## 5. Evidence and limits

- **Local contract and code checks:** `research/data/scientific-contract.json`, `experiments[id=EXP-03]` (`physical_ecap_observation_validation`), `ECAP-OBS-01`, `STOP-H2-B`; `research/service/integrity.py` enforces four ordered machine-contract IDs and `research/tests/test_scientific_contract.py` asserts the machine-contract EXP-03 name. These checks do not validate correspondence with the five TODO items. Prose: `research/docs/scientific-contract.md` §§2, 5–7.
- **EXP-01 dependency:** `EXP-01-H1-drosophila-protocol-draft.md` defines proposed synthetic-individual generation and matched graph nulls, but is still open pending NOV-05, source/model version bounds and protocol review.
- **EXP-02 dependency:** `EXP-02-ECAP-forward-measurement-spec-draft.md` defines source-specific measurement chain, acquisition and identifiability; EXP-02 remains open pending EXP-01, authorized independent data and numeric criterion preregistration.
- **Graph-null precedent:** S741, *Topological Sensitivity in Connectome-Constrained Neural Networks*, arXiv:2604.04033, Methods §§4.1–4.4, §5.3, results §§6.2–6.5 and limitations §10. Its graph-null comparison supports control design only; it is not evidence for ECAP transfer. Its graph scaffold version is not yet pinned in the local evidence records.
- **Matching cautions from primary methods:** S741 Table 1 and §§4.2, 4.4, 5.2 report matched model parameter counts, Adam learning rate 10⁻³, and an early 5/10-step matched-update probe. Do not import that unusually short horizon as a convergence budget. Its 734 trainable-parameter scaffold has 45,669 nodes and 1,513,231 edges; these FlyVis dimensions are not the EXP-03 architecture specification. No reviewed primary comparison validates the proposed human-only, naive-synthetic or shuffled-dynamics arms for ECAP; those remain protocol-defined counterfactuals requiring implementation review. S744 shares its FlyVis lineage, while S745/S286 provide only other-animal/connectome control precedents.
- **Participant split review:** local contract ENT-05 defines pulses nested in configurations, sessions and participants; the split unit is the participant, with a separate setting-cell holdout when claiming unseen stimulation settings. S765/S105 share an NCT04938245 cohort-family concern, though exact participant overlap remains unconfirmed. S789 and Gmel et al. 2023 (DOI 10.3389/fnins.2023.1297814) use the same 20-patient study cohort. Do not count these papers or their repeated observations as independent cohorts. Their accessible sample counts and reported CV schemes are not project validation data.
- **Dynamics-shuffle review:** the first-cycle literature audit found no direct primary precedent combining a fixed connectome, a temporal-order shuffle, exact marginal preservation and a dynamic-information readout. S741 is a graph null, not a dynamics null; S740 shuffles a derived statistic and graph weights, not model temporal states. Candidate neural surrogate methods preserve different things (rate, ISI, spectrum, zero-lag correlations); the chosen null and manipulation check must state those invariants explicitly. Treat the shuffled arm as a proposed test, not a sourced standard.
- **Human measurement and repeated-unit limits:** S105 (DOI 10.1088/1741-2552/adbfbe), S765 (10.3389/fpain.2023.1072786), S767 (10.1088/1741-2552/aceca4), S787 (10.3389/fnins.2021.673998), S789 (10.3389/fnins.2021.625835); see source-specific locators and caveats in EXP-02 and `research/data/ecap-scs-audit.json`.
- **Access gate:** `research/data/human-ecap-scs-access-audit.json` currently records `no_owner_confirmed_usable_dataset`. Without authorized participant-linked recordings, the human-only comparison and human-derived acceptance calibration cannot run.
- **Open gates:** EXP-01, EXP-02, RES-05 and EXP-04; NOV-05 remains open. Until these close, this document is a draft protocol only; do not launch costly simulations or claim either experiment is complete.
