Subject: Research data access inquiry — NCT04938245 ECAP signals and patient-linked outcomes

Dear Drs. Darrow and Herman,

I am reviewing human spinal cord stimulation data for a research protocol on ECAP measurement and clinical outcomes. The NCT04938245 registry describes a future release of preprocessed anonymized data and currently lists the study as recruiting, with estimated primary completion on 31 December 2026. Its primary and secondary endpoints concern feasibility and acceptability; correlation between ECAP signals and pain relief at two weeks is an exploratory other outcome. Related publications include König et al., *Journal of Neural Engineering* (2025), DOI 10.1088/1741-2552/adbfbe, and the earlier two-participant recording-method study by Ramadan et al., *Frontiers in Pain Research* (2023), DOI 10.3389/fpain.2023.1072786. Please clarify whether either publication's participants overlap the promised deposit.

Could you confirm whether a research dataset is available now, either by direct request or through a repository? In particular, could you provide or describe:

1. The data dictionary and unit of observation, including whether raw or artifact-cleaned ECAP waveforms, pulse-level features, stimulation settings, electrode geometry, and timestamps are available.
2. Whether these measurements and pain-relief or other clinical outcomes share a stable pseudonymous participant identifier, and the outcome definitions and follow-up times.
3. Who controls the data and may authorize its release? The Ramadan et al. article states that its two-participant data are Abbott proprietary and confidential; does that restriction apply to any part of the NCT04938245 release, or require Abbott's separate approval?
4. The consent and IRB scope for independent reanalysis, required application or data-use agreement, permitted uses, and secure delivery procedure.
5. The repository accession or expected release date, license, whether the two-week exploratory clinical outcome is complete and locked, and the relationship between a request-based dataset and the planned Zenodo deposit.

If analysis-ready data become available and governance permits, the proposed study would compare participant-level prediction and calibration of ECAP neural recruitment using human-only and connectome-constrained Drosophila representation models, plus prespecified degree-preserving rewired-connectome controls with shared initialization, shuffled-dynamics controls and naive-synthetic controls. A separate clinical-outcome analysis would be considered only if suitable participant-linked scores and follow-up times are available and reuse is authorized; the current two-week exploratory outcome alone would not establish a long-term clinical prognosis. A full protocol, analysis plan and institutional data-security details can be supplied for eligibility review. No clinical benefit or cross-species transfer is assumed. Please do not send identifiable patient information by email.

Sincerely,

[Researcher name and affiliation]
