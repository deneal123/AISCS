# Draft data access inquiry — Saluda Medical EVOKE and ECAP study cohorts

Status: **draft; not sent**. Prepared 2026-09-25 from the sponsor-authored EVOKE and ECAP study statements and ClinicalTrials.gov records. Submit through the Saluda Medical contact/data-request route only after the researcher adds identity, affiliation, institutional email and an approved protocol.

**Subject:** Request for participant-level ECAP and clinical outcome data — NCT02924129 and NCT04319887

Dear Saluda Medical data-sharing team,

I am assessing human SCS datasets for an independent research protocol on epidural ECAP measurements and clinical pain outcomes. The publications for the EVOKE trial (NCT02924129) and the ECAP study (NCT04319887) describe a qualified-researcher data request process, while the ClinicalTrials.gov records list IPD Sharing: NO. Could you clarify whether either cohort can be considered under the current data-sharing process?

For each cohort separately, please confirm:

1. Whether a request is currently eligible and whether any ongoing or planned regulatory submission limits sharing.
2. The available data dictionary and unit of observation: raw ECAP waveforms, pulse-level features or summaries, stimulation settings, lead/electrode geometry, timestamps, patient-reported pain measures and follow-up visits.
3. Whether ECAP/device measures and pain outcomes are linkable with a stable pseudonymous participant key, including missingness and cohort/visit inclusion counts.
4. The participant consent and IRB/ethics restrictions applicable to independent secondary analysis, and whether the proposed research purpose is permitted.
5. The proposal and statistical analysis plan requirements, DUA terms, permitted users/outputs, access duration, secure environment or transfer method, and any fees.
6. The relationship between the article's request process and the registry's `IPD Sharing: NO` statement.
7. Whether NCT02924129 and NCT04319887 share participants or overlap other EVOKE/real-world publications, and which legal Saluda entity can authorize release for each cohort.

If access and governance permit, the proposed retrospective analysis would compare participant-level prediction and calibration of ECAP neural recruitment using a human-data-only baseline and a representation initialized with connectome-constrained Drosophila dynamics, plus prespecified degree-preserving rewired-connectome controls with shared initialization, shuffled-dynamics controls and naive-synthetic controls. Clinical pain outcomes would be analyzed separately only if suitable participant-linked scores and follow-up times are available and reuse is authorized. The evaluation would keep participants separate across train and test sets; no clinical benefit or cross-species transfer is assumed. A full protocol, analysis plan and data-security details can be supplied for eligibility review. Please do not send identifiable patient information by email.

Sincerely,

[Researcher name]
[Institution and department]
[Institutional email]

## Evidence boundary

The analysis paragraph is a proposal derived from the working dissertation concept (`docs/dissertation-concept.md`, status `G0_REVISE`), not an approved protocol. The articles establish that cohorts with ECAP/device measures and clinical outcomes were studied and describe a request route. They do not confirm that pulse-level or patient-linked records are included in the shareable analysis datasets, that this project's proposed reuse is within consent, or that a request would be approved. The conflicting registry statements remain unresolved until the data owner responds.
